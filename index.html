Questions

أسئلة
Do I need any equipment or experience?

No. Bring yourself and your guests. Microphones, cameras, lights and an operator are included.

What do I get at the end of a session?

Your raw multi-camera video and audio the same day. With The Episode you also get a full edit, three vertical clips and a thumbnail within five working days.

How many people can be on camera?

It depends on the space. The Solo Corner is made for one person; the Majlis Lounge and the Desk seat a host with guests. Tell us your line-up and we'll suggest the right space.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before your session. See our cancellation and refund policy for the details.

Where are you, and is there parking?

28B Street, Al Quoz Industrial Area, Dubai, in the same building as OUDCRAFT. Valet parking at the door is free.

Do you work with brands and agencies?

Yes. The Series and The Original packages cover recurring shows and full productions. Message us and we'll plan it with you.
